**Figure 4: Within-V1 functional heterogeneity provides a benchmark for differences among mouse higher visual areas.** A1) Anatomy-based registration of 32 MouseV2 V1 probe-location estimates from eight animals to the Zhuang et al. (2017) common retinotopic map. Colors identify the four categorical probe locations (B, C, A, and E). The source geometry comprises 16 realized probe locations, eight intended penetration targets, and eight approximate opening centers. A2) OpenScope imaging schematic over a widefield retinotopic map. The twelve imaging locations are shown in three equally represented colors (four locations per color) that are spatially interleaved for visualization and do not define three anatomical groups. B) MouseV2 session-level time to first spike (TTFS), log10 F1/F0, and response-timescale estimates at the four V1 probe locations. Points are recording-session means, faint half-violins show their distributions, and colored bars and whiskers show the mean and normal-approximation 95% confidence interval. Probe locations are treated as categorical sampling locations, not as positions on a hierarchy axis. C) Corresponding session-level estimates from five cortical higher visual areas in the Allen Brain Observatory Neuropixels dataset, positioned at their published inter-area hierarchy scores. Colored points are session means, open circles and whiskers are area means and 95% confidence intervals, and dashed lines are least-squares fits through the five area means. Text reports the post-V1 slope and its session-resampled 95% bootstrap interval; TTFS and response timescale increased with hierarchy score, whereas the log10 F1/F0 slope interval included zero. D) Direct contrast in stable identity effects, Δω² = ω²(HVA) − ω²(V1), where ω² is the bias-corrected fraction of variance in session-level estimates explained by HVA or probe identity. Dots and labels show the observed contrasts and horizontal whiskers show 95% intervals from bootstrap resampling of complete session vectors, preserving the groups recorded within each session. All three intervals included zero. E1–E2) Single-neuron response-timescale distributions within V1 and across HVAs. Kernel-density ridgelines are weighted so that each session contributes equally and each neuron contributes equally within its session; repeated metric draws from a neuron share that neuron's weight. Vertical lines mark weighted medians, annotations give unique-neuron and session counts, and the displayed range is limited to 0–150 ms. The post-V1 analyses include LM, RL, AL, PM, and AM; LP is excluded.
