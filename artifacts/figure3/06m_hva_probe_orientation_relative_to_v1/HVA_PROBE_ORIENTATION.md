## Interpretation

A consistently positive delta across HVA-primary probes means these probes fan OUTWARD from V1 as they descend -- their surface entry sits relatively closer to V1's own footprint, and the electrode tip lands farther out in HVA/associated territory. A negative delta would mean the opposite: probes converge toward V1 at depth despite entering farther away at the surface.
