# Iteration 0 — pipeline baseline

_Frozen 2026-08-04; no scientific definitions changed._

## Result

MouseV2 sessions. The three regenerated PNGs are pixel-for-pixel identical to
the products preserved before the rerun. Their file hashes differ only because
of PNG encoding metadata. The statistical Markdown is identical after ignoring
its generated-date line.

This establishes a reproducible visual and numerical checkpoint for subsequent
iterations.

metric and population issues listed below remain unresolved.

## Deliberately preserved limitations

- Preferred grating conditions pool spatial frequency.
- MouseV2 F1/F0 and the reference `mod_idx_dg` are not yet parallel metrics.
- Published RF significance and area filters are absent.
- Two figures omit MouseV2 QC while two outputs apply it.
- Probe positions are placeholders rather than measured RF centers.
- Targeted-probe units are assumed to be V1 and have no cortical-layer labels.
- Some TTFS displays use mean matching rather than independent calibration.
- The bootstrap does not preserve the within-session probe vector.
- LP is included among post-V1 areas.
- CCG remains a one-session smoke test and is outside this baseline bundle.

## Bundle contents

- `pre_rerun/`: products that existed before the 2026-08-04 rerun.
- `rerun/`: products regenerated by the unchanged scripts.
- `run_manifest.json`: code, input, output, repository, environment, seed, and
  known-limitation provenance.
- `metric_counts.csv`: counts for every session, probe, and metric under the
  unfiltered and `default_qc` analysis profiles.
- `exclusions.csv`: exclusions by session, probe, metric, and reason.
